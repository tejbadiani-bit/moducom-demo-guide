Say this

"Today, what you know about Kent County is split across Excel, a network folder, and someone's inbox. Here it's one record — every contact, every deal, and every open support ticket in one place, and your whole team sees the same thing."

Point out

  • Contacts — the operations manager, the executive director, with roles and direct lines.
  • Opportunities — the active deals for this authority (e.g. the $240K dispatch-position expansion).
  • Cases — their open support tickets, right alongside the sales relationship.
  • This is the "centralize everything" outcome — no more hunting across systems.

Click path

Cases → the ticket queue → open 00001038 (NG 911 ALI data)

Say this

"You asked for a ticketing system — this is it. Every support issue a dispatch center reports is a Case: logged, prioritised, owned, and tracked to resolution — and it's tied to the same customer record your sales team works from."

Point out

  • The queue — trunk failover, ALI data drops, recording timestamps, radio-gateway backup failures.
  • Priority & status — high-priority 911 issues surface first; every ticket has an owner.
  • Linked to the account — support history and sales history live on the same customer.
  • This is the Service Cloud half — the "one system" that also covers support.
